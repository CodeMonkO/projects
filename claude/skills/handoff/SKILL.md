# Engineering Handoff
## Objective
Preserve decision-quality context, not conversation history.

## Include
- **Objective** — what are we trying to achieve?
- **Completed** — what was actually implemented/investigated?
- **Decisions** — important decisions and why.
- **Changed Files** — important files and purpose.
- **Verification** — checks run and results.
- **Current State** — what works and what does not?
- **Unresolved** — known problems and unanswered questions.
- **Risks** — important technical/product risks.
- **Next Action** — exact next logical step.

Do not fill the handoff with conversation history or speculative future work. A new engineer should be able to continue from it alone.
